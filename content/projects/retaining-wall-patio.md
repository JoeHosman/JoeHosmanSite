## The project

Over about a year in 2022–2023, I rebuilt a retaining wall and built a patio and
firepit area. To carry out the work, I bought a mini-excavator and a Bobcat.

The old railroad-tie walls were deteriorating, and contractor quotes prompted me
to look into doing the work myself. I learned how to inspect used equipment,
bought a Bobcat 331 mini-excavator and a 453 skid steer, and learned to operate
them on the project.

## The machines became projects, too

Owning the equipment brought another set of skills into the build. I had to
learn how to rebuild hydraulic cylinders and work on a diesel engine.

That included chasing hydraulic leaks, replacing cylinder seals, and replacing
a track. The equipment needed attention throughout the job, so repairs became
part of the construction schedule.

## Moving and placing the material

I moved gravel and limestone blocks into the backyard with the skid steer and
placed the blocks with the excavator. By my estimate, the project used about
120 tons of limestone, with individual blocks weighing around 1,000 pounds.

My cousin made drone surveys before and during the work, giving me 3D point
maps of the changing site. I owned the skid steer for a year and sold it for
what I had paid once its part of the job was finished.

The project included both the outdoor space and the mechanical work that came
with the equipment needed to build it. I completed the retaining wall and
patio/firepit project in 2023.
