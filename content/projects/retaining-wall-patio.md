## The project

Over about a year in 2022–2023, I rebuilt a retaining wall and built a patio and
firepit area. To carry out the work, I bought a mini-excavator and a Bobcat.

## The machines became projects, too

Owning the equipment brought another set of skills into the build. I had to
learn how to rebuild hydraulic cylinders and work on a diesel engine.

The project included both the outdoor space and the mechanical work that came
with the equipment needed to build it. I completed the retaining wall and
patio/firepit project in 2023.
