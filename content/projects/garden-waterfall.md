## Building with what people were giving away

My wife and I built a waterfall in the front garden, reusing a pump, liner, and
many of the rocks from people removing their own backyard ponds. Water collects
in a reservoir at the bottom and circulates back to the top, flowing over a
series of small falls.

It's shallow, with no fish, but aquatic plants and frogs have settled in.
Butterflies, bees, and other pollinators visit as well.

Keeping the water in the system has been part of the work: leaks and evaporation
need attention. The [lake-fed irrigation project](/projects/lake-water-irrigation/)
also supplies water to this part of the garden.
