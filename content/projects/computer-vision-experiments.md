## Getting the ideas off the screen

Online courses in computer vision and machine learning led me to experiments
with character recognition, grouping data, and following moving targets. One
involved putting a camera on a remote-control car and having it track and follow
a target.

These were learning experiments. They gave me a way to connect image processing
to the behavior of a physical machine, alongside the image-recognition work I
was doing in software.

I later participated in a [TreVia](/career/trevia-digital-health/) project using
computer vision to highlight areas of retinal images for human review.
