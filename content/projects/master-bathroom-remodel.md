## The project

Over about a year and a half in 2024–2025, I remodeled my master bathroom down
to the studs. I removed walls and handled the design, electrical wiring,
plumbing, and tiling myself.

## From layout to finish

The work brought several trades together in one room. My involvement extended
from designing the space to doing the work behind the walls and installing
the tile.

I completed the remodel in 2025.
