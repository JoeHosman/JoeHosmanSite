## Putting old hardware back to work

A friend gave me a full equipment rack, and many of my old computers have found
their way into it. The lab supports shared storage and media around the house,
as well as a place to experiment with Docker, Kubernetes, and other tools.

I use a software firewall for network routing, security tunnels, and VPNs. Guest,
family, and work devices have separate network access, while a NAS provides
shared storage for the household.

The lab changes as I try things and reuse hardware. It's an ongoing project
rather than a fixed installation.
