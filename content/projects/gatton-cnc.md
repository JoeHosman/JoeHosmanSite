## A tool for the next project

While planning woodwork for my kitchen remodel, I wanted a way to make more
involved carvings and shapes. I found plans for a Gatton CNC machine, cut the
wooden parts, sourced the motors and other components, and built the machine.

I've used it for planing work and cutting a specific sloped angle. Inlays are
something I want to try next; the machine is built, while that work is still
ahead of me.
