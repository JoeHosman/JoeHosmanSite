## Before this lived in a phone

For my senior project at DeVry, a classmate and I built an alarm clock around a
small computer. It had a screen and buttons for setting alarms and calendar
events, with different schedules for different days and an integrated AM/FM
radio.

It connected to a wired network so it could be controlled remotely. My dad
helped arrange fabrication of a very substantial metal enclosure through the
shop at his workplace.

We got the system working and earned an A. The enclosure was overbuilt, but the
project brought software, electronics, and physical construction together.
