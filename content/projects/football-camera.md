## The view from the ball

A client approached [Hosman Solutions](/career/hosman-solutions/) with an idea:
put a camera inside a football and cut to its live view during a pass. We took
on the experiment and built prototypes with embedded cameras.

## Where the experiment stopped

The changing spin made the video hard to watch. We tried different approaches
to correcting the rotation, including mathematical transformations and rendering
tools. With some post-processing time, we could improve the footage, but we
didn't achieve the required real-time correction.

The hardware also added weight and changed how the football felt and flew.
The project remained a prototype; it didn't become the live camera system the
client had envisioned.
