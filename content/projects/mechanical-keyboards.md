## From design files to a working board

I wanted to try the whole assembly process, so I started with an open-source
keyboard design and ordered manufactured multilayer circuit boards. Two friends
came over, and we assembled the keyboards together.

We did both through-hole and surface-mount soldering, fitting the processors,
LEDs, and other components before programming the boards. Each of us ended up
with a working mechanical keyboard.

The board design came from the open-source project. Our work was taking that
design through fabrication, assembly, and programming into something we could use.
