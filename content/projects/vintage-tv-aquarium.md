## Giving the cabinet another life

In 2007, I found a nonworking Hoffman television at an antique shop in Kansas.
The wooden cabinet was worth saving. I removed the old television hardware,
disassembled what I could, and stripped and refinished the wood while keeping
the front glass and controls.

I added wheels and a hinged top, then built a plexiglass aquarium to fit inside.
Opening the top gave access for feeding and maintenance.

## Making the old tubes glow

I reused vacuum tubes as housings for waterproofed, color-changing LEDs. A
microcontroller outside the tank slowly changed their brightness and color,
making the old components appear to glow again.

I also experimented with an LCD panel in front of the aquarium, using the tank
as a backlight. It was difficult to see through, so that experiment didn't work
as well as I had hoped. The aquarium itself housed fish for quite some time.
