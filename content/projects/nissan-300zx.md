## A repair that became an education

My Nissan 300ZX had a serious clutch problem. Working through it taught me how
to remove the engine and drivetrain components and replace the broken clutch
fork pivot bolt. When the two-piece driveshaft failed, I replaced it with a
performance single-piece shaft.

I also experimented with ECU programming and made custom fiberglass subwoofer
enclosures, starting with a wooden frame and stretched fabric before adding
resin and fiberglass.

I owned the car from my college years until around 2015. Much of what I learned
came from having a problem in front of me and working through the repair.
