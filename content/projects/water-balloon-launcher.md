## Following the idea

I built a water-balloon launcher and then used it as a platform for experimenting
with sensors, radio communication, and software-controlled movement. The work
brought together mechanical construction and the challenge of translating
measurements into physical behavior.

We also considered a self-loading mechanism, but never built it. Much of the
apparatus was lost or damaged through subsequent house moves. This is a record
of the experiment; I'm keeping the construction and targeting details private.
