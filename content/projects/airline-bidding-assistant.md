## Watching for the right opportunity

An airline employee approached me about the time spent watching for desirable
flight assignments. I built a C# application that connected to the bidding
website and monitored opportunities matching their preferred days.

The tool assisted with the bidding workflow, but the person using it made the
final decision. It reduced the repeated checking and preparation while keeping
that choice under human control.
