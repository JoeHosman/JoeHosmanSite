I'm Joe Hosman. I build software, lead engineering teams, and spend a fair amount
of my spare time making things with wood, electronics, and machinery. I like
understanding how something works well enough to repair it, improve it, or build
it myself.

## How I work

Much of my career has started with someone describing a frustrating process:
inventory that sells twice, a document review that depends on unwritten rules,
or a customer issue that nobody can reproduce. I work with the people doing the
job, figure out the rules, and turn that understanding into software.

At [StarSoft](/career/starsoft-systems/), that meant creating Ticket Attendant
and running the technology operation of a SaaS business serving about 500
customers. At [Performance Contracting Group](/career/performance-contracting-group/),
it meant growing a development team from 7 to 14 with no developer turnover
during my time as manager, then moving into a role focused on technical
architecture. At [Imperial PFS](/career/imperial-pfs/), it meant working through
document-validation rules with the people who knew them and coordinating the
automation with an outside vendor.

I have a deep C#/.NET background, with work across web applications, databases,
distributed workers, cloud services, and the infrastructure underneath them.
Testing and troubleshooting are a large part of that work. At
[Stratus](/career/stratus/), I built tools for reproducing customer issues,
understanding test coverage, and using AI-assisted development with human review.

## Starting things

I was one of three co-founders of
[How to Adventures](/career/how-to-adventures/), a site for sharing practical
adventure guides and finding places to explore. I handled technical architecture
and brought in remote developers to help build it.

Through [Hosman Solutions](/career/hosman-solutions/), I've taken on projects
including freight-bidding automation, an airline employee's bidding assistant,
and a tool for converting 3D point data between incompatible formats. Some
engagements were experiments: a camera inside a football produced usable
prototypes, but we didn't solve the live video stabilization problem.

## Where I started

In high school, I attended Summit Technology Academy's advanced networking
course. That led to first-level technical support work at KU Med and Unity
Village, answering calls, troubleshooting problems, and repairing and cloning
computers. In a VICA computer-applications competition, I placed first at the
regional and state levels and fifth nationally.

I later returned to help judge student programming competitions. A friend and I
also organized technology fundraisers for Summit Tech; I handled much of the
publicity, sponsorship, and event operations.

I earned a BS in Electrical Engineering from DeVry University. While in college,
I built a web application for a construction company to replace large, copied
Excel estimating sheets with searchable assemblies, parts, and packages. My
senior project, built with a classmate, was a
[networked alarm clock](/projects/networked-alarm-clock/) with programmable
schedules and an AM/FM radio.

My later study included non-credit online courses through MIT and Stanford in
machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous driving. I put that interest
into practice through image-recognition work and
[camera-guided experiments](/projects/computer-vision-experiments/).

## Away from the keyboard

I built a [Gatton CNC machine](/projects/gatton-cnc/) for woodworking, assembled
and programmed [mechanical keyboards](/projects/mechanical-keyboards/) with
friends, and restored an old wooden television cabinet as an
[aquarium](/projects/vintage-tv-aquarium/). Other projects have included car
repairs, fiberglass fabrication, and a microcontroller-controlled kegerator.

Rebuilding the [retaining walls and patio](/projects/retaining-wall-patio/)
at home became an education in operating and repairing heavy equipment. My wife
and I built a [garden waterfall](/projects/garden-waterfall/) largely from
reclaimed materials, and I've been building a
[lake-fed irrigation system](/projects/lake-water-irrigation/). Indoors, there's
usually something to try in the [home lab](/projects/home-lab/), alongside
Raspberry Pi sensors and a BirdNET bird-call monitoring project.

Scouting is part of my background. I enjoy camping, being outdoors, and leaving
places better than I found them. I'm also a member of the Lions charity group,
whose work includes helping people with vision needs.
