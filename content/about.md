I'm Joe Hosman, a hands-on technology leader and architect with thirty years of
industry experience. I turn customer needs and business workflows into software,
automation, and distributed systems.

My career has included creating and commercializing Ticket Attendant, owning the
technology operation of a SaaS business serving about 500 customers, leading a
development team as it grew from 7 to 14, and designing enterprise document
automation. I work across architecture and implementation, with a deep C#/.NET
background and experience in Azure, GCP, and AWS.

## Beyond the day job

My interests span process automation, machine learning, woodworking, mechanical
engineering, and art. My projects include a multi-camera bird-call monitoring
pipeline using BirdNET, a Git-backed multiplayer narrative game, and a published
Godot RPG where equipment behavior comes from supervised neural-network training.

I also experiment with a Docker/Kubernetes home lab and Raspberry Pi sensors,
mentor a team of Godot developers, and volunteer in high-school technology
mentorship and competition judging.

## Background

I hold a BS in Electrical Engineering from DeVry University. My continuing study
has included remote coursework in AI, machine learning, computer vision, and fuzzy
logic through MIT and Stanford programs.

This site brings those threads together: a record of my career, the projects I
take on, and what I learn along the way.
