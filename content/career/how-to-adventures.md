## Helping people find their next adventure

I was one of three co-founders of How to Adventures. We wanted people to share
practical guides: where to go, what to bring, what to expect, and what they had
learned along the way. The idea also included working with adventure destinations
and equipment businesses to help people discover those experiences.

## My part in the build

I handled the technical architecture and brought in remote developers to help
with implementation. We built an Angular website backed by MongoDB and
Elasticsearch, with guides presented as community-written posts.

Location was central to discovery. People could search by place name, street
address, or latitude and longitude. We built an address-to-coordinate cache to
support those searches and connect written guides to places on the ground.
