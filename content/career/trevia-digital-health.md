At TreVia Digital Health, I worked on a diabetes-care portal that brought together
exam scheduling, access to results, clinician messaging, and third-party
healthcare information integration.

I also extended the referral application's language support through localization
across its AngularJS frontend and .NET API.

## Repeatable delivery

CruiseControl.NET into the development workflow.
