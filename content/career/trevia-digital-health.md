## Patient self-service

At TreVia Digital Health, I worked on a diabetes-care portal that brought together
exam scheduling, access to results, clinician messaging, and third-party
healthcare information integration.

I also extended the referral application's language support through localization
across its AngularJS frontend and .NET API.

That included English and Chinese language support. This was my first substantial
work with AngularJS and single-page applications.

## Connecting the camera to the care workflow

I enhanced the Java application that watched for retinal images from a camera
and communicated with the web application through WebSockets. The work connected
image capture on a local machine with the application used in the care workflow.

I also participated in a machine-learning and computer-vision project to flag
potential issues in retinal images for human review. The purpose was to help a
technician focus their attention; clinical decisions remained with people.

Working with protected health information made security and compliance part of
the day-to-day engineering context.

## Repeatable delivery

I established automated builds, deployments, and Jasmine unit tests, integrating
CruiseControl.NET into the development workflow.
