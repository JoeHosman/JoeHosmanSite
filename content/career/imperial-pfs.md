## From business workflow to working system

At Imperial PFS, I worked with business and technology leaders to translate
operational needs into software and automation. I collaborated directly with the
CIO and VP of Engineering on engineering roadmaps and communicated product
progress and technical direction to executive leadership.

One substantial effort was redesigning insurance-premium loan document
validation with department leadership. I owned discovery, architecture, and
vendor delivery using UiPath and intelligent document processing. The redesign
reduced document-validation labor costs by at least $300,000 a year.

Much of the difficult work happened before implementation. Reviewers knew how
to recognize altered documents and exceptions, but many of those rules existed
only in their experience. I worked through that knowledge with the department
so the vendor-delivered automation could reflect the actual acceptance process.
The goal was to reduce manual review and free people for other work.

## Integrations and automation

- Led API design and implementation with C#, .NET, and microservices to support
  customer-facing integrations and data ingestion.
- Developed the File Drop Integration proof of concept to simplify customer
  file submissions. It earned positive client feedback on usability and reduced
  manual processing.
- Delivered a contract-processing automation pilot within a fixed budget,
  replacing manual workflow steps with third-party integrations.

## Supporting delivery

I also strengthened the engineering practices around these systems: automated
testing, CI/CD, containerization, dependency checks, and coordinated Angular and
.NET upgrades.

I participated in data-lake design and vendor selection, software and framework
evaluations, and security and compliance reviews. I gave monthly automation
status updates to executive leadership and helped evaluate approaches to QA
automation, including UiPath and mabl.

Working with the senior data architect, I co-wrote a database optimization and
redesign roadmap. I also contributed to the roadmap for successive .NET
upgrades, separating longer-term modernization plans from immediate delivery.
