About this experiment

The seed fixes the sequence of choices. The generator checks turns, speed, height, and clearance, then picks from the pieces that fit. More weave weight favors crossings. If the track gets stuck, it backs up and tries another piece.

Switch to Playerclip to see the collision wedges. The bot rides these surfaces as the track grows, and failed runs can rule out a piece. Its movement uses a simplified Source-style model; a successful bot run still needs human playtesting.

This is a track-building prototype, with no player controls or Hammer export yet. Some seeds and settings stop short of the requested length. Use the ramp inspector to see where the choices ran out.
